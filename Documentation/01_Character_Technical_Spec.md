> **The character parameters live in one file, consumed by both engines:**
> `UnityProject/Assets/Resources/Content/character_spec.json`
> `SceneBuilder`/`CharacterBuilder` (Unity, C#) and `web/src/char/builder.js` (WebGL) both read that JSON. Change a number once → the character changes in both builds. This is deliberate: it guarantees *cross-build visual consistency* of the character, which is the single most important art constraint in this project.

### Authenticity / labelling rules (non-negotiable)
* This is a **stylised digital reconstruction of a real historical person**, built as an educational avatar.
* It is **not** a photograph, a scan of a real object, or an official portrait. Every in-game instance of the character carries the label **“Digital Reconstruction — artistic visualisation, not an authentic photograph.”** (see `CreditsScreen` and the archive record `arc_legacy_ambedkar_bio`).
* No facial caricature, comedy distortion, fantasy costume, or political branding is permitted. The character is portrayed in **formal period dress** in a respectful, neutral, museum-narrator register: calm, upright, scholarly.
* No quote, document title, date or event is generated by the art pipeline. All text comes from the curated content database (`Assets/Resources/Content/*.json`) with a `source` field on every record.

## 1. Reference analysis (the ten things that must survive translation to 3D)

Derived from the supplied-sheet brief + standard likeness references. Ranked by recognition weight — if any of items 1–6 is wrong, the character is not recognisable.

| # | Feature | Observed in reference | 3D translation rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Eyewear** | Round, dark, thick **rimless/semi-rimless** round lenses, thin dark temples, close-set to the face | Torus-rim (2) + lens disc (2) + temple bar arms (2). Lenses slightly oversized (Ø ≈ 0.055 m) to read at gameplay distance. **Must never be removed or hidden.** |
| 2 | **Hair / hairline** | Short, neat, dark-near-black hair; **receding hairline**, higher forehead, volume at the sides above the ears | Skull-cap hair shell driven by `hairlineHeight`; side mass > top mass; no fringe; no long hair. |
| 3 | **Head & face** | Rounded, full face; broad cheeks; soft chin; medium-dark warm brown skin; **small clipped moustache**; straight, level brows | Lathe/ellipsoid head, cheek scale widened on X, jaw shortened. Moustache = 2 tapered boxes just under the nose. Neat, not bushy. |
| 4 | **Build / silhouette** | **Stocky, broad-shouldered, barrel-chested**, short-ish neck, substantial midsection; upright formal stance | Shoulder width = 2.35 × head width (heavy build); torso expanded by `bellyScale`; **no heroic/athletic taper**, no body-builder V. |
| 5 | **Suit** | Formal **three-piece** in muted colours — dark navy/charcoal and grey/olive variants appear; single-breasted, notch lapel, usually buttoned | Jacket shell (torso + upper arms), **waistcoat** band, notch lapels as flat angled plates, flap pockets, satin-ish sheen. Default palette = deep navy; grey variant available. |
| 6 | **Shirt + tie** | **White shirt**, high collar, and a **red patterned tie** (small repeating motif) | Collar ring (white, brighter roughness), tie = tapered 4-segment strip with a **procedural micro-pattern texture** (dots/diagonal motif) in crimson with cream/charcoal accents. Tie is **on top of** the waistcoat/jacket gap. |
| 7 | **Trousers** | Same family as jacket, wider leg, straight fall over the shoe | Tapered cylinders, cuff breaks on the shoe; palette = darker, desaturated companion to the jacket. |
| 8 | **Footwear** | Dark brown/black **lace-up leather oxford/derby** | Bridged shoe block with toe bevel + heel; leather roughness ~0.42; subtle heel highlight. |
| 9 | **Stance** | Still, formal, hands relaxed or one hand holding a book; minimal gesturing; head slightly forward | Idle = low-amplitude breathing (≤2° chest), slow head micro-motion, weight evenly set. **No exaggerated idle bounce, no swagger.** |
| 10 | **Associated objects** | Books, folders, manuscripts, spectacles-handling | A **carryable book prop** parented to the left hand bone for the *reading / carrying / examining* clips, hidden for locomotion clips where it would clip. |

### Explicit non-goals (rejection list)
Anime/cartoon proportions, superhero musculature, fantasy or futuristic clothing, comedy moustache/glasses exaggeration, youth-slider drift, "generic businessman" palette swaps, any change of costume without a narrative reason. The only permitted costume variation is **palette** (navy ⇄ charcoal-grey) and the documented **period-appropriate** cuff/collar change; silhouette stays fixed.

## 2. Body proportion table (metres, 1 Unity unit = 1 m)

Total height **1.700 m** with shoes (character reads as compact and solid, hairline slightly below default humanoid eye-line scaling). All values are *drivers* in `character_spec.json`.

```
Y  1.700 ─ hair top              ┌──── head capsule (ellipsoid)
Y  1.618 ─ eye line              │     headHeight 0.236  · headWidth 0.176
Y  1.575 ─ nose / glasses        │     headDepth 0.196
Y  1.545 ─ mouth / moustache     │
Y  1.507 ─ chin                  └──── neck: 0.086 tall, r 0.062, slight forward pitch 4°
Y  1.452 ─ shoulder line         ┌──── shoulderWidth 0.414 (2.35 × headWidth)  ← stocky read
Y  1.212 ─ elbow                  │    upperArm 0.246  · lowerArm 0.238  · hand 0.098
Y  0.975 ─ wrist                  │    torso: chestD 0.242 → waistD 0.252 (barrel, not tapered)
Y  0.940 ─ waist (jacket button)  │    waistWidth 0.332 · bellyBulge +0.014 fwd
Y  0.856 ─ hip                    └────
Y  0.470 ─ knee                   ┌──── legLength 0.856  · thigh r 0.092 → calf r 0.068
Y  0.062 ─ ankle                  │    shoe: 0.262 long · 0.104 wide · 0.078 tall
Y  0.000 ─ floor / sole           └────
```

**Head-to-body ratio:** ≈ **1 : 7.2** (realistic-educational register — *not* stylised 1:5.5 cartoon).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head group | head, neck, ears ×2, nose, brow, moustache ×2, chin | identity | ~1 900 | 900 | 380 |
| Eyewear | lens ×2, rim ×2, temple ×2, bridge | identity | ~780 | 420 | 180 |
| Torso group | jacket, chest, waistcoat, shirt, collar, tie ×4, lapels ×2, pocket flaps ×2, buttons ×4 | silhouette | ~2 300 | 1 100 | 420 |
| Arms group | upper ×2, elbow, lower ×2, hand + 5 fingers ×2 | interaction | ~2 100 | 900 | 340 |
| Legs group | thigh ×2, knee, calf ×2, trouser cuff ×2 | locomotion | ~1 500 | 700 | 260 |
| Footwear | shoe ×2, toe ×2, heel ×2 | grounding | ~620 | 300 | 120 |
| Prop | closed book | reading/examining | ~120 | — | — |
| **Total LOD0** | | | **≈ 9 300 tris** | ≈ 4 300 | ≈ 1 700 |

Texture strategy: **no external texture files are required.** Character materials are flat PBR colours plus three *procedurally generated* 256² textures:
`tie_pattern` (repeating motif), `fabric_weave` (subtle noise for jacket), `leather_grain` (shoes). These are generated at load time (`ProceduralTextures.cs` / `textures.js`) — offline-safe, no licensing risk, tiny memory.
Skin uses a 256² vertical gradient ramp + pore noise for tonal variation instead of a UV-mapped face texture, because the character is a *digital reconstruction avatar*, not a photoreal likeness.

## 5. Rig specification (humanoid, Unity-Mecanim compatible)

Bone list generated by `CharacterBuilder` (every bone is named so `Animator` can retarget):

```
Root
└ Hips
  ├ Spine → Chest → UpperChest → Neck → Head
  │                              ├ Jaw (unused, reserved)
  │                              └ Eye_L / Eye_R (static, reserved for look-at)
  ├ Shoulder_L → UpperArm_L → LowerArm_L → Hand_L
  │                                     └ Thumb_L Index_L Middle_L Ring_L Pinky_L
  ├ Shoulder_R → UpperArm_R → LowerArm_R → Hand_R
  │                                     └ Thumb_R Index_R Middle_R Ring_R Pinky_R
  ├ UpperLeg_L → LowerLeg_L → Foot_L → Toe_L
  └ UpperLeg_R → LowerLeg_R → Foot_R → Toe_R
```

* 4-finger + thumb chains exist so the **pointing / note-taking / page-turn** animations are possible (§9 of the brief).
* Bind pose = A-pose, 12° arm drop, so both idle and lean-forward clips interpolate cleanly.
* `Head` bone drives camera-independent **look-at** during exhibit examination.
* Skinning: automatic weights with a manual review pass on the shoulder and hip joints (the two places automatic weights fail on a stocky build).

```
         Dialogue    →  { Speak, Listen, Greet, Think, Explain, Lecture }

            InteractType (int: 0 none,1 press,2 read,3 examine,4 sit,5 book,6 notes) ·
            DialogueMode (int: 0 none,1 speak,2 listen) · Trigger, DoorTrigger, SkipIntro (trigger)
Blend:   Locomotion = 1D blend on Speed (Idle→Walk→Run) + 2D strafe blend on (MoveX, MoveZ)

             IK: hand IK on exhibit grip points, foot IK on slopes (feet locked, no sliding)
```
Root motion is **disabled**; movement is kinematic from `PlayerController`, animation follows — this keeps Android touch movement predictable and prevents door-transition desync.

## 7. Materials / shader setup

* **URP Lit** for all character parts (Universal Render Pipeline, mobile-safe).
* Shader keywords: `_SPECULARHIGHLIGHTS_OFF` unused; `_ALPHATEST` off; glass lens uses URP Lit with **Surface Type = Transparent, Alpha 0.22, Render Queue 3000** and `_Smoothness 0.92`.
* One shared `CharacterMaterialSet.asset` (ScriptableObject) holds the 15 materials so a palette swap is a single asset edit.
* Mobile override (LOW preset): skin/jacket/cloth roughness +0.08, lens alpha +0.06, shadows = single directional + no additional-lights pass. The character must never be the reason a mid-range Android device drops frames.

## 8. In-engine build path (no external DCC tool, no downloaded model)

The character is **built by code** so the pipeline is reproducible, license-clean and diffable:

```
character_spec.json  (numbers, colours, materials, bone table)
        │
        ├── Unity:  Tools ▸ Heritage ▸ Character ▸ Rebuild Character
        │            Assets/Editor/CharacterBuilder.cs
        │            → Prefabs/Characters/AmbedkarCharacter.prefab
        │            → Art/Characters/Ambedkar_LOD0/1/2.prefab
        │            → Animations/Ambedkar_*.anim (24 clips)
        │            → Art/Animations/AmbedkarAnimator.controller
        │
        └── Web:    web/src/char/builder.js  (THREE.BufferGeometry + THREE.Skeleton)
                    identical parameter table → visually matching character
```

**Why code-built instead of a downloaded FBX:** the brief supplies only a character sheet; any third-party model would be a licensing risk and would drift from the sheet. A parametric builder guarantees the §1 identity features, stays editable by the team, and gives us LODs and collision proxies for free.

### Integration checks (per brief §54, step 11)
The character is validated from **five camera configurations** by `Tools/validate_character.js`:
`front` · `side` · `¾` · `gameplay` (third-person, 3.4 m behind, 1.9 m high) · `inspection` (1.2 m, eye level).
Each capture is compared against the §1 checklist (glasses visible ✔ · tie visible ✔ · hairline ✔ · stocky shoulder ratio ✔ · palette within ΔE 6 ✔).

## 9. Acceptance criteria (definition of done for the character)

1. Glasses, moustache, receding hairline and red patterned tie are visible in a 1080p gameplay shot at the default 3.4 m camera distance.
2. Shoulder-width : head-width ratio = 2.35 ± 0.05 (the "stocky" read).
3. No fantasy/cartoon drift: proportions are 1 : 7.2 realistic; no oversized head or hands.
4. All 24 clips exist, transition without popping, and no foot sliding on the locomotion blend.
5. Character reads correctly on Android LOW preset (silhouette preserved, glasses still legible).
6. The "Digital Reconstruction" label is present wherever the character is presented as a historical figure.
7. Changing one number in `character_spec.json` and rebuilding changes **both** builds identically.
